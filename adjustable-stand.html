A custom 3D printed stand that suspends 0.25–1 inch diameter piping at adjustable heights and drops straight into the benchtop's 1 inch hole grid. I designed and iterated through three height-adjustment mechanisms and many slider revisions to land on a stand that is easy to adjust with one hand and holds up over time.

Project Overview

The lab needed a way to suspend piping from 0.25 to 1 inch in diameter at various heights, integrated with the 1 inch spaced holes in the benchtop. I designed and 3D printed a series of stand iterations, each one aimed at improving overall print quality, how easily the stand could be adjusted with one hand, and how well it would hold up over time.

Height-Adjustment Mechanisms

I tried three different ways of adjusting height: a pinch slider, a rack and pin system, and a one-way push slider. The pinch slider shown above became the final design; these are the alternatives that were built and tested along the way.

Slider Iteration

Every slider, first to last. Laid out in the order they were printed.

The slider is the part a user actually touches, so it went through the most revisions. Each version was printed, fit into the frame, and tested for how easily it adjusted and how securely it held before the next change was made.

  • Slider widthTuned so the slider moves smoothly in the frame without wobbling.
  • Pinch area sizingSized so the slider can be squeezed and moved with one hand.
  • Snap-fit ledges & groovesAdjusted so the stand locks firmly in place and won't fail, while still being easy to release.

Print Quality

The final pinch slider geometry was printed in two orientations. Orientation changed the quality of the finished part, so the better orientation was used for the final stand.

Good orientation. Final pinch slider with clean print quality.
Bad orientation. The same slider geometry with noticeably worse print quality.

Snap-Fit Details

The frame uses slits with ledges and grooves that the slider snaps into. Getting these dimensions right was the balance between a stand that holds its height reliably and one that is still easy to adjust.
