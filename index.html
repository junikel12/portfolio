About

I am a sophomore Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Maryland and the Solid Propulsion Co-Lead on the Terrapin Rocket Team, where I lead a group designing and constructing a 30,000 ft O-class motor. We use Fusion CAD and CAM, FEA, CNC mills, lathes, drill presses, and software like NASA CEA and OpenMotor to design, test, and fabricate every part of the motor.

I also have experience conducting thermal-fluids research at both the NEIT Lab at the University of Maryland and the Laboratory for Advanced Thermal and Fluid Systems at Villanova University. Further, I was elected to serve as an undergraduate delegate to the Clark School Engineering Senate, where I am using this role to improve the experience of all engineering students at the University of Maryland.

Project 01

Cold Plate Cooling Test Loop

Designed an experimental cold plate cooling test loop to evaluate how gyroidal cold plates improve heat dispersion and affect pressure in a liquid cooling system. I selected components such as pumps and tubing based on the project requirements, then optimized the placement of every component within the provided benchtop area to minimize total tube length and the number of bends, and to use gravity to maximize pump efficiency. I modeled the entire 200+ component system in SolidWorks and produced a Bill of Materials with costs, links, and justifications.

Project 02

Adjustable Pipe Stand

Designed and iterated a custom adjustable stand that suspends 0.25–1 inch diameter piping at various heights and mounts directly into the benchtop's 1 inch hole grid. I tested three height-adjustment methods — a pinch slider, a rack and pin system, and a one-way push slider — and iterated on slider width, pinch area sizing, and snap-fit ledges and grooves to improve print quality, one-handed usability, and long-term durability without making the stand hard to adjust.

Project 03

Autonomous Over Terrain Vehicle

The goal of this project was to design a fully autonomous Over Terrain Vehicle (OTV) capable of completing tasks at a mission site while navigating through an obstacle course. I was responsible for the drivetrain and bracket design systems of the OTV. To select motors and wheels, I completed drivetrain calculations focused on torque, speed, and budget constraints. I also utilized SolidWorks, Fusion360, and Onshape to design and 3D print custom brackets for motors, wheel axles, sensors, and movement arms integrated throughout the vehicle.

Project 04

Full Battle Droid Assembly

Developed a fully detailed Battle Droid Assembly in SolidWorks using reference images and mechanical assembly design. I created custom modeled geometry and joint systems using spline tools, advanced extrusions, and dimension refinement to replicate and customize the original B-1 Battle Droid design. Through iterative modeling and problem solving, I transformed reference imagery into a realized physical CAD assembly emphasizing realism (especially the head), articulation, assembly integration, and detailed component interaction.
