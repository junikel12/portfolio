Project 04

Lightweight
Phone Stand

A weight-optimized phone stand designed to hold a cellphone both vertically and horizontally while coming in under a 20 gram mass constraint. Final mass: 19.38g.

Project Overview

The goal of this project was to design a phone stand that weighed under 20 grams and could hold a cellphone both vertically and horizontally. I first designed this stand to be much taller and much more filled in. After doing so I was significantly over the weight requirement so I cut out non-necessary material, created fillets, and reduced the height as much as possible to ultimately weigh in at 19.38g.

Physical Model

The stand was 3D printed and tested in both vertical and horizontal phone orientations. The final print confirms the geometry holds the phone securely in either position.

Front view — printed in PLA, showing the full frame and foot geometry.
Side view — showing the angled support brace and reduced cross-section.

Finite Element Analysis

FEA was performed in SolidWorks Simulation to verify structural integrity under phone loading. Both Von Mises stress and displacement results were analyzed to confirm the design would not yield or deflect excessively under normal use.

Von Mises Stress — stress distribution showing peak stress concentrated at the base brace junction, well within material limits.
Displacement — maximum deflection occurs at the top of the stand, confirming acceptable rigidity under load.

Engineering Drawing

A fully dimensioned engineering drawing was produced in SolidWorks, documenting all critical dimensions, tolerances, and the final mass of 19.38g.
